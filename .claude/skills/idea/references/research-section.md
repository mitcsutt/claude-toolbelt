# Research section template

Append to the issue body, after `### Public repo check`. Keep it under about 60 lines.

```
### Research

_Added by Claude on <YYYY-MM-DD>. Everything above this heading is the author's own words._

**Prior art and existing tools**
- <tool or write-up> - <what it does, how it overlaps> (<link>)

**Feasibility**
<one short paragraph: what is hard, what is easy, what is unknown>

**Options**
| Option | Tradeoffs | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <option> | <pros / cons> | <S / M / L> |

**Recommendation**
<one option and why; say what would change the recommendation>

**Not verified**
<claims without a primary source, or checks skipped>
```

Rules:
- Cite a link for each factual claim; list the rest under **Not verified**.
- Options include "do nothing" and at least one that fits no existing plugin.
- Prefer primary sources: docs, repos, specs.
